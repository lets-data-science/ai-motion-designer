# AI Motion Designer · design contract

The memorable element is a real, seekable motion canvas. Keep the editor quiet.

- Paper #ffffff, ink #171717, canvas #000000, focus #ffd02f, muted #6e716a, divider #e6e6e3.
- System sans for interface; locally available sans fallback for the video. No remote fonts or images.
- A full-width header with the same white LDS logo as AI Walkthrough Studio.
- Desktop: large canvas and scene strip on the left, one selected-scene inspector on the right. A collapsed brief sits above. Mobile: canvas first, scene strip next, inspector below.
- No ornamental gradient, tilted mockup, fake metrics, floating decorative card, or auto-playing sound.
- Real motion connects labeled objects: tokens arrive, a pipeline transfers focus, two alternatives separate. A single seekable timeline drives preview and render.
- Square video corners, no outlined video frame. Timeline selection and form controls use restrained borders because they encode state.
- Play is deliberate. The paused initial frame is useful. Reduced-motion users keep control of playback.
- All source examples are labeled illustrations. The AI plan is a draft awaiting factual review.
